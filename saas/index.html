FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. The Solo plan is free forever for one user with up to three active projects.

Can I import data from spreadsheets?

You can import clients, projects and past time entries from a CSV file in a few minutes.

Which payment methods can my clients use?

Clients can pay invoices by card or bank transfer through supported payment providers.

What happens when my trial ends?

You choose a plan or move to Solo automatically. Nothing is deleted.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Plans are month-to-month unless you choose yearly billing, and you can export your data at any time.
